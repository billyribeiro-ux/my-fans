# Operator, creator and subscriber workflows

MyFans supplies three application experiences. The company uses Administration and the owner-only operating commands; creators use Creator Studio and Earnings; subscribers use discovery, checkout, content, messages and Settings. The general [operator policies](operator-policy.md) cover each party. External vendors retain their own account agreements and approval requirements.

## Transitions completed in the October 8 review

- Creator progress is available only to the signed-in owner. It contains verification/capability states and observation times, without provider account IDs, applicant IDs or secrets. Refresh shows the server's last observed state; a hosted return does not establish approval.
- Rejected creator profiles can be resubmitted after verified email and recent MFA. Concurrent retries converge on one pending review and one immutable resubmission record. The reviewer can see the submitted explanation. Approval remains a separate decision with its existing identity requirements.
- New creator review messages are explicitly disclosed as creator-facing in the reviewer UI. Historical internal moderation reasons are not copied into that field. Application erasure and recovery replay clear these current messages; restricted moderation evidence follows its captured retention rules.
- Past-due subscriptions can be canceled truthfully after provider confirmation. Cancellation preserves the recorded status, paid period and entitlements; duplicate requests do not create duplicate local cancellation events. Terminal or unresolved initial states are not offered an invalid cancellation action.
- Hosted checkout returns to Settings or the home page. Return-query text is informational and does not grant paid access.
- Settings includes an owner-scoped, paginated payment history. Every Stripe renewal receipt is separate; an original checkout intent is not counted again once receipts exist. Refund amounts and a historical dispute marker are shown without claiming the current dispute outcome. Payment references support investigation without exposing provider identifiers.
- Shared post links resolve one canonical post, including posts older than the first feed page. Publication, creator eligibility, blocking and paid access checks still apply; unavailable links show an explicit empty state. Creator discovery controls open the paginated directory.
- Message drafts belong to their recipient thread and are discarded when the signed-in identity changes or the dialog is disposed. Switching threads cannot send the previous recipient's draft accidentally.
- Report reviewers use a separate, audited, recent-MFA inspection view. It can display restricted or removed evidence and private captioned video without changing payments, subscriptions or entitlements. Public post links retain ordinary member access rules; media requests independently recheck assurance.
- Account mutations revalidate the current session and required assurance after acquiring the account lock. Freeze, password recovery and MFA recovery serialize against these writes. Provider calls preserve successful external identifiers for reconciliation while withholding new hosted capabilities when the initiating account or session is no longer eligible.
- Operator policy reloads verify replacement web and worker deployments and the resulting public policy. Recovery checkpoints retain exact historical task revisions and immutable images, allowing isolated historical restoration after a newer release has been deployed.

## Operating responsibility and remaining inputs

The operator must supply its real legal identity and contacts, adopt the policy/retention/eligibility choices, assign people to support, moderation, finance, privacy and incident response, and supply approved hosting/payment/identity/mail account information. Provider-hosted onboarding, bank settlement, tax configuration, actual mailbox delivery and cloud failover must be exercised in those accounts. These outcomes cannot be established by local fixtures.

The implemented workflows and configuration are ready for that account setup. Current artifact identities and observed test results are recorded in [verification](verification.md). Follow [production activation](production-readiness.md) and [AWS runtime](aws-runtime.md) to activate the configured environment.
